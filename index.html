Hi!

I’m Dong, a physical oceanographer, currently a PhD student at University of East Anglia. My research focuses on understanding the ocean circulation and its interaction with climate variability. I use a combination of observational data and numerical modeling to investigate ocean-ice-atmosphere interactions. I am also interested in operational polar oceanography, novel observations, deep-sea, etc.

  • Have a look at the Nordic Seas Ocean Circulation
  • Interactively check out Water Masses in the Nordic Seas
